# Comparative Interpretation Output Protocol

Use this format when the user requests a deep comparative reading. If they asked casually, keep it concise; do not force every lens. Never pad the output with frameworks that do not fit the dream or lack a relevant source.

## 1. Method and disclaimer

Say plainly that dream interpretation is exploratory, not a validated universal decoding system. Explain that empirical findings, psychoanalytic theories, cultural/religious beliefs, mythic/literary readings, and speculation will be labeled separately. The dreamer is the authority on personal associations. Dreams cannot establish pregnancy, diagnosis, prophecy, another person's feelings, or hidden memories.

## 2. Dream as reported

Summarize only details the dreamer provided. Identify unknowns (especially emotion and personal associations); do not fabricate.

## 3. Scientific / research lens

Use [science-framework.md](science-framework.md). Include the most relevant empirically supported possibilities, their confidence, limitations, and what science cannot infer from this report. Avoid saying “science says this symbol means…”

## 4. Requested interpretive frameworks

Choose relevant headings from [comparative-frameworks.md](comparative-frameworks.md), plus modules Freud, Jung/Red Book, Bible, and Tolkien when requested. For each, explicitly state its classification:

- **Empirical science:** observation/result, evidence strength, uncertainty.
- **Psychoanalysis:** named theorist/school, historical/clinical theory, not universal fact.
- **Religious/philosophical tradition:** source and tradition, accurately described as belief/text/practice; metaphysical validity not independently settled by science.
- **Myth/folklore/literature:** documented narrative motif or literary analogy; not an authoritative prediction or clinical theory.
- **Modern esoteric/pseudohistorical:** identify a modern text or movement by author/date where known. Label unsupported empirical claims (e.g., aliens in ancient Mesopotamian texts, purported Atlantean provenance) as pseudohistorical/pseudoscientific when asserted as factual history/science; do not use “pseudoscience” to dismiss religious belief or literary symbolism.
- **Speculation:** a possible personal reading, contingent on dreamer's associations.

For each framework: (a) state what the framework actually claims, (b) connect only relevant reported details, (c) offer one or more tentative readings, (d) specify limits. Do not make every tradition interpret every symbol if no source supports it. Keep viewpoints distinct before synthesis.

For user-requested Egyptian/Thoth, Enoch, or Anunnaki lenses, retain the specific boundaries in comparative-frameworks.md: distinguish Egyptian dream papyri from later Hermetic texts; the medieval Emerald Tablet from Doreal's modern *Emerald Tablets*; 1 Enoch's ancient Jewish apocalyptic vision from present-day prophecy claims; and Anunna/Anunnaki deities in ancient texts from modern ancient-astronaut narratives. Never attribute a precise symbolic meaning unsupported by a source.

## 5. Cross-framework common threads

Compare only interpretations actually presented. Use a table or bullets:

| Candidate shared theme | Which lenses support it | Type of support | How strong / caveat |

Distinguish genuine independent convergence from apparent convergence caused by shared source texts, cultural transmission, translation choices, broad wording, or the analyst steering each lens toward the same metaphor. A repeated theme across traditions can raise its value as a meaningful cross-cultural motif or a useful hypothesis; it does NOT make a metaphysical proposition “quite likely true.” Use “convergence raises salience, not proof.” A claim is more scientifically credible only when independently testable evidence supports it.

## 6. Differences Summary

Explicitly list where frameworks disagree, including:
- internal/personal meaning vs external/divine message vs narrative/philosophical metaphor;
- past/waking concerns vs present relational/social meanings vs future-omen claims;
- whether the dream should be interpreted at all;
- what evidence or authority each framework accepts;
- plausible alternative meanings that cannot be resolved from current details.

## 7. Joseph's Interpretation

Use this exact heading when requested. Synthesize only (i) shared motifs with source labels, (ii) research-supported observations, and (iii) the dreamer's own associations if known. Mark the conclusion as a personal integrative hypothesis, not a discovery of “veins of truth.” Separate: **best-supported observation**, **most useful psychological possibility**, **mythic/religious resonance**, and **unknown**. State what further dreamer context would change the reading. Do not privilege Joseph's interpretation over the dreamer's own view; if “Joseph” is not the dreamer, label it as an outside synthesis.

## 8. Close

End with one or two open questions about emotion and personal associations, unless user asks for no questions. Keep length proportional to requested depth.
